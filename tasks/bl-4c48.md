Ten `control`-classed ops with no interactable in this seat: `fleet`, `disband`, `arm`, `disarm`, `scan`, `science`, `work-diff`, `ops`, `ack`, `clear-trail`.

Two halves of one absence. The fleet verbs are how a workspace is made to run its ready balls and made to stop, and how the cheap-model watch over it is raised and dropped; the trail verbs are the record of every act that crossed the boundary, its alarms, and the truncation that acknowledges them. A seat that can start one conversation but not a fleet, and that cannot read back what it already did, is a face on half the engine.

Exemption rows in `parity.toml` cite this ball until the surfaces land.
