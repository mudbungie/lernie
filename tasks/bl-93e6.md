Diagnosed from a live yog session, 2026-08-02 (yog dev workspace agent
`20260802T223426Z-1277061f`, "energize"). Two prompt-side faults, one
deliverable: the `dispatch` skill text and the worker soul.

## 1. Agents burn paid steps polling for a dispatch result

After dispatching, energize spent **six consecutive steps** on shell sleeps:

    bash {"command":"sleep 2; echo waiting-for-subagent"}
    bash {"command":"sleep 3; echo checked"}
    bash {"command":"sleep 3; echo waiting-for-findings"}
    bash {"command":"sleep 5; echo checked-again"}
    bash {"command":"sleep 5; echo final-check"}
    bash {"command":"sleep 5; echo waiting-second-agent"}

(`messages/010`–`026`.) Six model calls, one per sleep, producing nothing.

The skill says the result "arrives on a later step as a message in your inbox"
— but never says that **ending the turn is how you wait**, and the model has no
way to infer it: from inside the loop, ending the turn looks like giving up. The
deposit revives a quiescent agent (§2.11), so parking is free and polling is
pure burn. Say so in the skill, in the imperative: do not sleep, do not poll —
end the turn; the deposit wakes you.

## 2. A worker handed `dispatch` delegates instead of working

The operator asked energize to have a subagent analyse yog's XDG tree. What
happened (agent-id descent shows the depth):

    …-2962f775  →  …-1591b815  →  …-0c789cd1  →  …-1cf7d465

Four levels, each generation dispatching rather than looking. The level-1 child
ended its turn — a `final-response` terminal, its whole contribution to the goal
— with:

> Spawned a subagent to inspect Yog's XDG paths and files safely. I'll report
> its findings when it finishes.

The parent had to message it back ("Do not merely say another subagent was
spawned"), and in the end did the work itself. Two gaps: the worker soul does
not say the goal is yours to *do* and delegation is the exception; and nothing
stops "I delegated" from being a legal terminal response to a goal, which is
what makes the regress self-sustaining.

## Deliverable

Skill/soul text — `skills/dispatch/SKILL.md` and the worker soul in `template/`:

- an explicit **wait** paragraph (end the turn; never sleep/poll; the deposit
  revives you), and
- a **do-it-yourself default** (the goal is yours to execute; dispatch is for
  work that is genuinely separable or parallel, not for passing the goal on) —
  plus, in the terminal contract, that reporting a dispatch is not answering a
  goal.

Worth attacking whether a depth or fan-out signal belongs in the harness rather
than the prose; prefer the prose if it holds, since a cap is a new knob.
