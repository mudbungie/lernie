The seat carries a gesture envelope and reads three things out of it (DESIGN §3.3): that it is an object with an `op`, which workspace it names, and whether the last reply frame said ok. That is enough to route and to exit; it is not enough to PAINT.

A window renders typed replies — a roster row, a conversation, a transcript step, a diff hunk, a ball. So the window needs the decode half of the reply vocabulary: the `Reply` kinds it draws, strictly decoded, refusing an unknown kind in band rather than defaulting.

Two rules ride with this and neither is negotiable. yog REMOTE is the protocol authority and a shared protocol crate was refused: the seat REIMPLEMENTS what it needs, exactly as the android client does. And it decodes only what it paints — a kind nothing renders is a kind nobody has to carry.

The honest question this ball must answer first is how much of the vocabulary the first window actually needs, because the whole of it is a large surface and most of it belongs to panes that do not exist yet. Answer that before writing a type.
