Split out of bl-9fd1, whose CLI half landed: `lernie stop <workspace> <agent> children` is the cascade, and `crate::verbs::stop` now takes the flag.

The window still fires the BARE form from both of its stop controls — the composer's act row (`src/ui/composer/acts.rs`) and the conversation row's menu (`src/ui/convs/menu.rs`) — and `src/ui/records/header.rs` prints the engine's own sentence, *the engine offers nudge, stop with its children*, beside no control that reaches the second half of it.

Measured in bl-9fd1: on a conversation with children the bare stop does not stop it. The root's driver dies, a child's driver deposits into it, and it is running again about five seconds later, having spent 796,522 further tokens. Reproduced on two conversations in two workspaces.

Why it was not done there. A cascade is a second control with a second confirmation and it belongs beside the records that say what is under there — DESIGN §4.20's arming, the shape `delete-agent`'s typed name already has. The menu is explicitly not the place: `src/ui/convs/menu.rs` states that nothing on it destroys and that its door signature is the admission test. So this is a pane's work, with its own parity tag and its own paint assertions, and smuggling it into a serialization ball would have been a pane built without one.

What it needs:
- an armed cascade beside the records pane's offers line (or wherever §4.20's arming pattern says), firing `crate::verbs::stop(workspace, agent, true)`;
- the offers line is already the read that says whether the engine is offering it (`reply::agent::Offer::Children`), so nothing new crosses the wire;
- a parity tag on the control, since `stop` is already surfaced and the ledger is per op — this adds no `parity.toml` row and removes none.

---

Built as the ball asked, with two departures worth recording.

The arming is an ENABLEMENT, not a parameter. The wire's stop carries a boolean flag and no typed field, so the seat cannot leave the arming a parameter the way delete-agent's is; it is a box this end holds, compares against the row's display name (whitespace forgiven, the engine's own rule) and never sends. DESIGN 4.20 is amended in the same commit, because as written it read as though destruction were what earns an arming: the test is scope. yog's 3.6 doctrine already says typed-name confirm iff the act reaches beyond the one named on screen, and a cascade does that while leaving history, worktree and inbox standing. Undoing one is nudge PER conversation, so 'undone by doing the other thing' is not the answer here either.

The pane could not take the control without giving up two line breaks first. At the narrow shape the records pane had under four points of slack: one wrapped row put a control off the bottom of the window and reddened the snapshot matrix. Swept the two halves that had not been swept to 4.32's own rule -- the steps heading now shares its line with its first fact, and the governing half's three facts share one wrapped row. Nothing the pane says was dropped. Recorded in 4.32 as the measurement it is, so the next control added here knows what it costs.
