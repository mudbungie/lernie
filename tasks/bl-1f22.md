Found by the canonical-scene drive (bl-2b7f), noted alongside bl-f6b5 and split off from it because it is a different mechanism.

## What the glass shows

For the first seconds after a start from the composer, the chat pane's header says "waiting to hear what this conversation is" (`src/ui/records/header.rs` `NOT_ANSWERED`) and the pane shows not even the deposit the operator just sent, until the first transcript read arrives.

## Why, read from the tree

Selecting the started conversation (`Model::select`, reached through the claim's resolve) retires the transcript to empty, and the transcript is only ever filled by the asker's next pass (`src/offframe/asker.rs` `tick`), which first asks every channel's roster (`everywhere`) before the focused reads. On a dialed wire a pass is seconds, so the selection waits up to a full pass plus the rosters ahead of it. bl-f6b5's fix (the live fold paints only in place of a Streaming entry) does not touch this: there is no transcript yet at all.

## Open question for the implementer

Whether the fix is ordering (the focused reads first, or a pass woken on a selection change) or the seat painting the goal it just sent as the deposit until the read lands. The second is a seat-side prediction of an engine fact and is probably wrong by this crate's own rules (the read is the authority); the first is scheduling. Decide in the ball.
