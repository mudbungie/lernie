Gesture: on the real window (X11, Xvfb, XTEST), press Ctrl+X anywhere — box focused or not.

Expected (DESIGN §4.42 item 7, keyboard whole): the switcher overlay opens from anywhere, the box included.

Observed: nothing. egui-winit 0.30 (on_keyboard_input) pushes egui::Event::Cut for command+X and returns before pushing the Event::Key; crate::ui::keys::handle's chord reads only Event::Key { key: X, modifiers.command }. The headless harness injects Event::Key directly, so every test passes while the real chord is dead.

Why it matters: with bl-fa26 (no box while nothing is aimed) the first frame of a fresh seat has no keyboard way in at all; the seat panel names Ctrl+X as one of its two doors.
