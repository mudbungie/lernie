The window (bl-428f) can message and nudge a conversation that exists. It cannot begin one, and that is the first thing an operator reaches for on a wall they have just aimed at.

Starting is two acts, not one (REMOTE §8.1): `prepare` answers a `prepared` body — the fire-time parameters — and `prompt` hands that body straight back with a goal. So the seat has to hold a reply between two gestures, which nothing it carries today does, and the second act is posted only when the first one's receipt arrives.

What it costs:

- The `prepared` reply kind, in the seat's own vocabulary. It is the largest single payload on the reply surface and the one whose fields are least obviously paintable, so the ball's first duty is the same one bl-4174 had: decide how much of it a first start pane actually needs, and record the rest as unpainted.
- The `started` receipt, whose product is the minted conversation name — and that name is a barrier: every gesture after it may address the conversation the reply just made addressable.
- A pane, and a decision about where it goes. The composer is already the place a conversation is spoken to; whether a start is a mode of it or a surface beside it is this ball's to settle.

`corpus/unreadable/unpainted-kind-prepared.json` and `unpainted-kind-started.json` are the two fixtures that move to `corpus/answers/` when this lands, which is what makes the ledger say the truth again.
