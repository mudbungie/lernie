The released engine (yog 0.0.30) speaks **13**; this build pins **8**, so the
preface refuses every connection by exact equality before a gesture is sent.
Five bumps to cross. yog's `src/wire/hello.rs` carries the ledger verbatim and
`docs/REMOTE.md` §9.16-§9.18 the reasoning.

## What moved between 8 and 13

| # | Shape | Move |
|---|---|---|
| 9  | `reply/steps` | **LOST** `auth_failed` |
| 9  | `reply/transcript` | rows gained `wound`, `wound_reason`, `auth_row` (the `wounded` entry) |
| 10 | `attention` | no field moved: the ask became follow-class, a sequence of frames |
| 11 | `reply/ops` | gained `failed`, `exit_label`, `standing` |
| 12 | `reply/attention` rows | gained `says`; `reply/acknowledged` is a new shape spelling rows the same way |
| 13 | `reply/config` | gained `settings` — the file schema applied to the bytes answered beside it |

Four ops are new and all four are classed `control`: `login`, `login-tail`,
`pin`, `unpin`.

## The one break

Everything above is a gain except one, and a gain reaches a lenient decoder as
nothing. `reply/steps` **lost** `auth_failed`, which this build reads with
`fields::flag` — required — so at 13 every steps reply fails to decode and the
corpus replay puts the fixture in the wrong class.

REMOTE §9.16 states the replacement outright: *"a seat that read `auth_failed`
reads `wound == \"refused\"` instead."* The affordance is the `refused` arm of
the wound vocabulary both shapes already spell, and this build already decodes
`wound` and `auth_row`. So the field goes, and `ui::records::auth` gates on the
wound class it was always a projection of.

## What stays in the ledger

`ops` and `config` are already `corpus/unreadable/` files here — their gains
cost this build nothing but a refresh. `reply/acknowledged` and `reply/login`
are new shapes and land in the ledger the same way. The `wounded` transcript
entry falls to `EntryKind::Unknown`, which is total by design; painting it is
bl-3257's, not this ball's.

## The act

Raise the pin to 13, `make corpus` from a yog checkout at that number, delete
`auth_failed` and re-point the affordance, and add the four parity lines
(`login`/`login-tail` -> bl-e3c5, `pin`/`unpin` -> bl-7782).
