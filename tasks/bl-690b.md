The advisory landed in the RustSec database on or about 2026-09-17 and `cargo deny check advisories` now fails on main's own lockfile, so every `bl close` on this repo is refused by the delivery gate. The manifest pins `rustls = "0.23"` and the fix is inside that range: `cargo update -p rustls` to 0.23.45 or later. Lockfile only; no manifest change, no new crate.
