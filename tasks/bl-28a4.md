(2) The boundary's prompt gesture with a prepared body (the second half of start) has no typed spelling except inside `start`, so a seat cannot re-fire a staged body it holds. ---

Half (2)'s premise is wrong on today's tree. A seat CAN re-fire a staged body it holds: 'prompt' is an ordinary envelope, 'lernie ask' carries any envelope verbatim, and envelope::slot_mut routes it by the workspace one level down inside 'prepared' and re-addresses it into the host's spelling on the way. Verified against a live scratch engine -- the hand-written fire was dispatched and answered with the engine's own refusal about credentials, not about the envelope. So DESIGN 4.10 states it rather than a word being added: the nested object is what has no typed spelling, which is 4.10's opening rule, and what 'start' adds is the local between the two acts plus the goal JOIN. That join is the real residual and is now stated: a prompt typed by hand off a path-rung prepare carries no target preamble.

Half (1) is real and fixed. 'ops' is a door now with an optional depth defaulted from crate::verbs::DEPTH, the same constant the window's trail pane asks with.

Also fixed a stale count 4.10 carried: it said the gesture table trails the roster by exactly four gestures held in two modules; there were five in three (trail.rs's ops was never counted). Replaced with the modules and no number.
