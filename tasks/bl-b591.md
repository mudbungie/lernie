docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §4.4 ("Attempts and retry — the harness's job") states verbatim:

> Backoff is purely config-driven — the exponential schedule in `workflow.yaml`, keyed off the attempt number — and consumes no provider pacing hint: the pinned brazen `CanonicalError` is `{kind, message, provider_detail}`, carrying no `Retry-After` / `retry_after_seconds`, so there is no hint to honor. (A pin-version fact, not a permanent stance: were a later brazen to surface a pacing hint in-band, the harness could clamp its backoff to it.)

The premise is false and has been for at least two pins. `brazen::CanonicalError` carries a fourth field in BOTH published 0.0.3 and 0.0.4 (registry source, src/canonical/error.rs:32):

    #[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none")]
    pub retry_after_seconds: Option<u32>,

documented there as "The provider's `Retry-After` pacing hint in WHOLE SECONDS (RFC 7231 §7.1.3), parsed from the non-2xx response HEADER — the transport-level fact a caller-owned retry loop wants".

So the hint EXISTS in-band and the harness ignores it. The doc's own parenthetical says that case is exactly when "the harness could clamp its backoff to it" — so this is not a typo to patch but a decision to make:

1. honor it (clamp the config-driven backoff to `retry_after_seconds` when present), and rewrite §4.4 to describe that; or
2. deliberately ignore it, and rewrite §4.4 to say so and why (backoff policy is the harness's, not the provider's) instead of asserting a field that is not there.

Noticed during bl-8c92 (pin bump 0.0.3 -> 0.0.4). Not changed there: the bump was mechanical and this is a stance, not a fact-correction.
