## The sighting

The installed seat at 0.1.4, connected to a protocol-6 engine on the same box
(yog 0.0.9, over the loopback wire), paints a **giant black box in the middle
of the app**. Reported by the operator against a running window, not against a
test.

## Why it is filed before it is understood

Three classes are open and they have different remedies, so naming one early
would be guessing:

1. **A paint regression between 0.1.3 and 0.1.4.** The window's own code moved
   in that window, or did not, and that is checkable rather than arguable.
2. **A pane whose data source changed shape under the protocol-6 re-vendor**,
   rendering an empty or failed state as a filled rect. This seat has exactly
   one surface that fills rects by hand rather than laying out widgets — the
   enrollment pane's QR symbol, which is defined dark-on-light and therefore
   draws real black at real size (`src/ui/enroll.rs`, `crate::qr`).
3. **A toolkit or texture failure** — the backend rather than the seat.

## The constraint on the evidence

The operator's desktop blocks screen capture, so a screenshot is not
available and is not worth pursuing. This repository's own answer to that is
the headless paint probe (`src/paint_probe/`): it renders a widget tree
off-screen and reads back every galley **with its ink and its rect**, which is
the fact this defect is about. A filled rect is not a galley, so the probe's
text projection alone cannot see it — the walk that carries ink and geometry
can, and any shape-level evidence this needs is an extension of that one walk
(`rules/no-hand-rolled-paint-walk.yml`: there is ONE walk, and a caller that
needs something else adds a projection to it).

Diagnose before fixing. If it does not reproduce headlessly, the deliverable
is the diagnosis plus one precise question for the operator, not a fishing
list.
