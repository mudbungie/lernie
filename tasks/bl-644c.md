DESIGN 4.46 item 3.

keys::BINDINGS: one table of every chord, what it does and the act it fires. keys::handle runs it; /help prints it where commands::KEYS stood (which goes); a control bound by a row cites the chord after its help sentence (keys::chord_for, read by the painters' callers). The rows added: Ctrl+, settings; Ctrl+B pin or unpin the bar; Ctrl+N begin a conversation on the aimed workspace; Ctrl+W close the selected tab; Ctrl+PageDown and Ctrl+PageUp the next and previous tab; PageDown and PageUp scroll the transcript a page from the box too; Ctrl+End and Ctrl+Home its ends; Ctrl+=, Ctrl+- and Ctrl+0 move the scale knob; F1 is /help. The eleven that stood keep their rows. A test per row fires the act through the harness.
