Landed by bl-55f9. src/prompt/inbox/tests/probe.rs, helper declines_until: the loop reads the driver log, breaks when it has seen enough declines, and otherwise sleeps 5ms. On a box where the child has already written by the first read, the sleep line never executes and tarpaulin reports 7284/7285 = 99.99%, failing the 100% gate; a re-run of make coverage on the identical tree passes at 7285/7285. Observed twice in one session on work/bl-c35d (bl-1966 alignment gate), once failing and once passing with no tree change. Fix so the line is deterministically executed rather than race-dependent (e.g. sleep-then-read, or drop the helper to a form with no conditional statement). Not a product defect - test-helper coverage only - but it makes every agent close flaky.
