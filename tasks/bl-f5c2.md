REMOTE §4.2: a certificate carries a grade, and a foot-grade leaf may advertise and execute and nothing else. A seat is operator-grade by definition — a face that could not ask is not a face.

Enforcement is the ENGINE`s, at the chokepoint where the client identity is already spent for scoping, and it is fail-closed and in band. So a seat configured with a foot-grade leaf is already refused, correctly, today. Nothing here is a security property.

What is missing is a DIAGNOSIS. That refusal arrives as an authorization sentence from the far end, when the fault is entirely this box`s own configuration — the operator pointed a seat at the wrong pair. Reading the grade off this box`s own leaf before dialling turns that into a sentence about the file on this disk. The sibling foot component does exactly this in the mirror direction and its walk is the model.

Deferred rather than dropped: it costs a DER walk over the subject, and the fault it improves is rare and already named by somebody.
