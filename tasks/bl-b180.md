## The ruling, and what is left of it here

The operator ruling the original exists for: on a wall holding no usable
provider credential, typing a goal and hitting Enter works zero percent of the
time — the conversation is born, immediately dies on no-models, and the operator
learns it from a dead row or from nothing at all. It was hit live, twice in one
evening, and both first goals were wasted.

The original ball was about WHICH boxes ask the rung: the server's start pane
asked it; its docked composer and its empty-world bootstrap box fired the same
doomed goal unguarded. Two of those three surfaces died with the window. What is
left is simpler and worse:

**this crate asks the rung nowhere, because it cannot ask it at all.**

Verified against both trees before filing:

- The seat's start box (`src/ui/composer/start.rs`) fires §8.1's start with one
  guard, and it is about the seat rather than the wall: a wall must be aimed at.
  Nothing about that wall's credentials is consulted, and no surface here could
  consult one.
- The argv start (`src/verbs/start.rs`, `src/seat/start.rs`) is the same act by
  the other door and equally unguarded — one box, one Enter, and the same
  outcome.
- The decision itself is written and tested on the engine (`src/start/gate.rs`,
  `StartGate` / `WallCredit`, that crate's DESIGN §8.1). Its only caller was the
  pane, so today it has none.

## What this needs

A boundary read, not a rung of this crate's own. `Query::Providers { workspace }`
is already boundary-complete on the engine and answers the provider rows the
gate reads, so the shape is: this crate's reply vocabulary gains that frame, the
model asks it for the aimed wall off-frame, and the start box paints the refusal
above the box instead of firing.

Three open questions, all seating rather than mechanism:

- **Whether the seat decides or the engine does.** The gate is a pure read of a
  table, and re-deriving its predicate here would be a second implementation of
  a settled decision — which VISION §8 refuses. Prefer the engine answering
  *ready, or not and why* over this crate re-judging rows; that may mean the
  engine grows the answer rather than the seat growing the predicate.
- **Noise.** The original's real design question survives unchanged. A band that
  stands for as long as the wall is unsigned is a different object from a
  one-line refusal at the moment a goal would be spent. This face is four panes
  wide and the composer is one control with two subjects, so a standing band is
  paid for by the pane beside it.
- **When the answer is not back yet.** The frame never dials (DESIGN §4.11), so
  the read is an off-frame ask like every other, and a start typed before it
  answers must not be silently swallowed waiting on it.

## Boundaries

Do not re-derive the gate's predicate. Its reasoning is settled and recorded in
the engine's DESIGN §8.1: a keyless row is not a credential (the built-in table
merges under every config, so a predicate they satisfy is one nothing ever
fails), and every other credential spelling readies the wall, including ones
this build has never heard of.
