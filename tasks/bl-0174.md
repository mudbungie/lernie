§4.45 item 5 as amended. A turn's working is every row between the operator's turn and its final prose. A running step stands whole; a finished call compacts to its call line and one elided line of its output, a finished thinking or intermediate prose to one elided line, each a door that opens it whole; once the turn settled the working is one line — its counts — that opens to the compact steps. Toggles are remembered as now; defaults decide where nobody toggled. The response elbow stands only while the working is open.

---

Default rule taken: the working is open while any of its rows is live, else one line; a step is in flight when it is a call without its result or the last row of the running turn (its output arriving, its prose streaming). Intermediate prose stands inside the working, whole on its own Intermediate fill inside the trail's, so item 7's four fills still all paint. A result whose call is not on the read counts as a call.
