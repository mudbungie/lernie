Round 1 measured two costs: Tab passes six window-level buttons before the first workspace, and Tab focus is not selection while arrow movement is, so one keyboard has two models of where I am. This ball makes the focused control visibly focused on the new visuals (a brand ring or tint that reads on the dark ground), keeps every control reachable by Tab and Space/Enter, and lets the arrows and Tab agree: the pane the arrows own wears the mark on its heading, and the row the cursor is on is the selected one. Asserted through the paint probe: the focused control's ink or fill differs from its rest state on the glass.
