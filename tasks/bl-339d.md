Gesture: /board Enter, then Ctrl+X, then Enter on a conversation (or Esc).
Expected (§4.42 item 6: one overlay stands at a time; this lane's bar: opening a second replaces the first): the switcher replaces the board; choosing a conversation shows that conversation.
Observed: the switcher paints over the board (only one is painted), and closing it — by Esc or by choosing — reveals the board again, still covering the conversation just chosen. Model::switching is a flag beside lookup/listing rather than one of them, so the two coexist. The slash words cannot reach this (the prompt stands down under an overlay); only the chord can.
