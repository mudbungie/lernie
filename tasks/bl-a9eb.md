Today `lernie ask` takes a gesture envelope as JSON on argv. That is the honest shape for a transport with no vocabulary: it carries what it is given and refuses what is not an object with an op.

Upstream the same verb takes a TYPED argv — the same reader the depositing seat uses, so the two cannot drift, plus the help whose subject is the interface rather than the world and which therefore answers without an engine being up.

This ball is that surface here, and it is gated on the vocabulary for the reason the window is: an argv reader produces typed gestures, and typed gestures are what it needs to exist first. What it must not do is grow a SECOND spelling of a gesture beside the envelope — one surface, two serialisations, never two implementations.
