docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §4.4 stated verbatim:

> Backoff is purely config-driven — the exponential schedule in `workflow.yaml`, keyed off the attempt number — and consumes no provider pacing hint: the pinned brazen `CanonicalError` is `{kind, message, provider_detail}`, carrying no `Retry-After` / `retry_after_seconds`, so there is no hint to honor. (A pin-version fact, not a permanent stance: were a later brazen to surface a pacing hint in-band, the harness could clamp its backoff to it.)

The premise was false: `brazen::CanonicalError` carries `retry_after_seconds: Option<u32>` in both published 0.0.3 and 0.0.4 (the `Retry-After` response header in whole seconds, RFC 7231 §7.1.3, parsed from the non-2xx headers; `None` where absent or unparseable — never fabricated).

## Decision (architect: Tumult) — HONOR the hint

The effective delay before the next attempt is `max(config schedule for that attempt, pacing hint)`. The provider's pacing hint is a **floor, never a shrink**; the `workflow.yaml` schedule otherwise governs unchanged. Absent hint → behavior exactly as before.

Rationale: retry *policy* stays workflow config's (§4.2 "Retry policy is not provider config at all"), while the one transport fact the provider is authoritative about is honored — a provider that says "wait 30s" knows something the schedule does not, and retrying sooner buys a second 429.

## Shipped

- `src/config/workflow.rs` — `Backoff::delay(attempt, retry_after_seconds)` is the single home of the effective delay: it computes the exponential rung, then `.max()`es it with the hint (`None` → `Duration::ZERO`, so the schedule is untouched). Pure function of the failed attempt's own error; nothing carried across attempts, nothing stored.
- `src/prompt/dispatch/model_call.rs` — the retry loop passes `err.retry_after_seconds` and sleeps only what `delay` returns. No new config, no new flag, no new vocabulary.
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §4.4 — the false claim and its parenthetical are gone. New paragraph **"Backoff: the config schedule, floored by the provider's pacing hint"** defines the **pacing hint** where it is introduced (per CLAUDE.md terminology discipline — no TAXONOMY coinage needed, and "pacing hint" was already §4.4's own phrase), states `max(schedule(n), hint)` and the three readings, and names the code home. §2.10 and the §6 `retry:` example point at it.

## Tests (three cases, twice — pure and wired)

`src/config/tests/workflow_yaml.rs` on `Backoff::delay` directly:
- `absent_pacing_hint_leaves_the_config_schedule_alone` — `None` → 250ms / 1000ms rungs.
- `pacing_hint_below_the_schedule_does_not_shrink_it` — `Some(0)` at rung 1, `Some(1)` at rung 12 (512s) both equal the no-hint delay.
- `pacing_hint_above_the_schedule_wins` — `Some(30)` → 30s at rungs 1 and 2.

`src/prompt/dispatch/model_call/tests/cases.rs::the_pacing_hint_floors_the_config_backoff` drives the same three readings through the real retry loop (a 429 `Error` segment carrying the hint, then a clean stream), asserting the duration the injected `Sleeper` actually recorded: absent → 250ms, `Some(0)` → 250ms, `Some(7)` → 7s.
