§4.45 item 5 as amended. A turn's working is every row between the operator's turn and its final prose. A running step stands whole; a finished call compacts to its call line and one elided line of its output, a finished thinking or intermediate prose to one elided line, each a door that opens it whole; once the turn settled the working is one line — its counts — that opens to the compact steps.
