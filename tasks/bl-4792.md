Operator ruling 2026-10-14 in seven parts: (1) the bar's linger after the pointer leaves goes from 1.5 s to 0.75 s; (2) both of the bar's widths, collapsed and expanded, are draggable; (3) every chat control moves under the box, so the box stands directly under the transcript; (4) a sent message appears in the transcript the frame it is sent, in an ink that says the engine has not acknowledged it yet — state is always represented, never a half-second where the message is nowhere; (5) a trail folds itself once its calls are done; (6) every clickable element lights under the pointer; (7) operator turns, trails, intermediate prose and the final answer each stand on a bubble of a slightly different fill, enough to know at a glance which is which. Design first (DESIGN §4.45), then the lanes, then a verification on the glass and an install.
