Each carries a minted name as memorable as the work's, each renders at the same weight, and each earns an `attention` row saying 'came to rest — your turn' — which is false: nothing is my turn about a finished compaction. Four of the five belong to ONE conversation.

The rows are not wrong: `depth` is 1 and the preview says what they are, so a reader who knows the vocabulary can tell. But the strip is the operator's index of what is happening, and after one session's work two thirds of what is happening is bookkeeping. A fork's candidate (`MauveBagpipe`, depth 1) is a conversation I DID start and it sits in the same undifferentiated run as four compactors I did not.

Expected, one of: compactors fold under the conversation they compact (they already name its branch, so the join is free); or they are filtered out of the roster and the attention queue by default, with the machinery reachable when asked for; or at minimum they do not raise attention rows, since there is no act an operator can take on a finished compaction.

Severity p3 as a rendering matter, and it is worth reading beside yog bl-52b7 (the compactor that spent 55 steps and 2.14M tokens): the roster is also where an operator would first notice a compaction going wrong, and today it is where they learn to stop looking.
