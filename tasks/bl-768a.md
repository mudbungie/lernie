Round-2 install lane, new scenario: a second box enrolled as both a seat and a foot using only typed verbs. lernie 0.1.33 (main tip 1763302), engine yog 0.0.46 at PROTOCOL 13 (the only pair that composes today — see yog bl-bca2).

GESTURE:

    lernie enroll home box2seat operator --into /<dir>/out-seat

WHAT IT DOES: exactly what it promises, and the act itself is good — the engine mints, registers and shreds, and the four files land with 0600 modes:

    filed as an entry in <dir>: ca.pem, client.pem, client.key, address. Carry the
    directory to the box it is for, under its own `wire/workspaces/<name>/`

WHAT IT ALSO DOES: before that line it prints, unconditionally, (a) a fifteen-row QR symbol and (b) the whole `{"yog-enroll":1,…}` envelope on one line — `ca`, `cert`, `address`, `grade`, `name` and `key`, where the `key` field carries the PEM private-key block in full, header line and all.

EXPECTED: `--into` is the operator saying "this box has neither a camera nor a paste box; write it down for me". Having said that, the key should not also go to stdout. It lands in the terminal scrollback, in `tmux` history, in `script`/`asciinema` captures, and in the job log of anything that runs this non-interactively — none of which the seat can shred, and all of which outlive the act. `lernie help enroll` states the design ("this seat says it three ways, because they are the same bytes") and the design is right for the bare call; `--into` is the case where one of the three ways was chosen.

SUGGESTED SHAPE (not a ruling): with `--into`, print the receipt line and a fingerprint, and not the symbol or the envelope. Bare, keep all three.

SEVERITY p2: not a defect in what the act performs, and there is a workaround (redirect stdout), but it is a private key written where the operator did not ask for it, in the one flow whose whole point is that the seat keeps nothing — the same sentence the command prints two lines later: "the seat keeps no copy".

Everything else about this verb passed. The two-box scenario worked end to end from typed verbs alone: enroll operator + enroll foot on the engine box, carry the two directories, `lernie workspaces` on the second box, `thrall run` on the second box, and a routed invocation ran there and came back with that box's own hostname in the capture.
