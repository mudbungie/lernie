Round-1 daily lane. lernie 0.1.30, window under Xvfb at 1440x900.

The conversation pane renders a tool result as flat verbatim text with no fold and no cap of its own. litany bounds a stream at 16 KiB head + 16 KiB tail before it reaches the transcript, so up to 32 KiB per call arrives here and all of it is painted.

Measured on two ordinary conversations:

- One `bash` running `pwd && ls -la && git log --oneline -20` filled the whole 830-pixel pane and continued below it: 27 lines of `ls -la` then 20 of `git log`. The user's own question and the model's reply are the two lines above it. `rounds/1/shots/daily/04-conversation.png`.
- One `bl --help; bl list --help` produced roughly 700 rendered lines. Steps 1 and 2 of that conversation occupy about six screens. `rounds/1/shots/daily/06-streaming.png`.
- A `find` over the home tree — 32,985 bytes — arrived essentially whole. `08-scrolled.png` is one screen of it: forty consecutive near-identical paths.

yog's own doctrine for the sibling surface is the answer already written down (STORIES S6 step 5, the activity accessory): "any row expands to argv, cwd, exit and stderr verbatim". EXPANDS — collapsed by default, opened on demand. The transcript's tool rows have no such state; they are always fully open.

The tool-use row above each result is already compact and well made (`claude-sonnet-5 ▢ bash toolu_…` then the input JSON on one line), which makes the asymmetry sharper: the call is one line and its answer is six screens.

Expected: a tool result renders as a header row plus a few lines, with the byte/line count and an expander — the litany marker already states "the original byte/line counts and where the full record lives", so the numbers exist. The Raw toggle and the records pane are where the whole thing belongs.

Severity p2: it is the reason the pane cannot be read, and it compounds the tail-anchor defect filed beside it — the content that pushes the answer off the screen is content nobody wants at that altitude.
