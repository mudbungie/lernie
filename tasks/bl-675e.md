Third bump in the release chain, after bl-d774 (2 -> 4) and bl-e6ee (4 -> 5).
The handshake is fail-closed with no negotiation (REMOTE §3), so a seat one
integer behind cannot dial at all.

## What moved (REMOTE §9.13, upstream bl-23bd)

**`reply/providers` rows gained `effort` and `priority`, two required
booleans** — which tuning knobs that provider row actually takes. Read as
columns out of the adapter's own provider listing and re-derived nowhere,
because only the row can see its own decline of a dialect key. They **gate a
control, never a write**: a row taking neither knob hides both selectors and
refuses no gesture, and they are required rather than optional precisely so an
unanswered question can neither block a row nor silently grant it a control.

Two new request ops rode along, `/effort` and `/priority`, and upstream is
explicit that they cost nothing: a new op is a new spelling in an existing
vocabulary and a peer that has not heard of one refuses it in band, by name.
**One bump, for the row.** The vocabulary is deliberately the operator's, not a
provider's — `effort` and `priority` because that is what the config says and
what a person means, never a dialect's own spelling.

## Readable-class outcome: nothing moved in a class this build paints

Verified rather than assumed, and the numbers are the verification.

- **Reply direction: no movement in `answers/` or `refusals/`.** The only reply
  shape that moved is `providers`, and it is one of the vendored files in
  `unreadable/` — the ledger. Checked three ways: `providers` is not among the
  reply kinds this seat decodes, so it falls to `reply::read`'s unknown-kind
  arm; its fixture is filed under `unreadable/` and the replay asserts exactly
  that class; and a search of the whole tree finds no model-pick, login or
  provider-reading surface for the booleans to be carried to. bl-e3c5 is the
  ball that would build one and it is unclaimed. So the §9.13 capability
  columns have no site, and inventing one would be inventing a pane — DESIGN
  §4.9: the ball that lands a pane is the ball that adds its kind.
- **Request direction: two ops added, both routed.** The vocabulary went from
  63 ops to 65. `src/verbs/tests/corpus.rs` replays every frame of every op —
  including the ones this seat has no word for — and asserts each routes by the
  address upstream's own signature says it carries. The 6 new frames (4 effort,
  2 priority) carry a top-level `workspace` and route by it, and they are
  genuinely exercised rather than skipped: the vendored-set test compares the
  files on disk against `shapes.json`'s own key list, so a fixture that failed
  to vendor would have failed that test rather than passing quietly.
- **`UNEMITTED` did not move.** Its counts are per op this seat EMITS, derived
  from the verb table; the seat has no word for either new op, so neither the
  emitted set nor the two recorded reasons changed.

So this is the second consecutive integer bought without a field.

## What was written instead

DESIGN §4.9's bump paragraphs were becoming a running log, which is the
restatement that rots. They are now one statement of the rule with the per-bump
ledger left at `PROTOCOL`, where it already lives — one fact, one home. Two
things are stated there that the mechanism cannot state for itself: that a
shape whose MEANING moves under an unchanged spelling is the one drift a corpus
replay cannot catch (bl-e6ee's `oid`), and that a bump reaches the WRITE
direction even when it paints nothing, because a gesture routed by a slot the
seat does not look in goes down the wrong channel silently.
