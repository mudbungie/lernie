Gesture: yog fixture busy on an isolated engine; Ctrl+X, pick the wall, pick any conversation (all five ask); type /trail (or /board, /records) and Enter.

Expected: the pane fills within a beat or two, as it does when no conversation is selected (verified: /trail and /board answer at once with only the wall aimed).

Observed: 'waiting to hear what has crossed the boundary' (trail), 'waiting to hear what is on the board', every records section, for tens of seconds. Traced with a temporary print in the asker: the pass reads workspaces, then attention (it stands while the selected conversation asks, §4.42 item 8, and while /attention is open), and attention takes ~30 s on this engine (yog bl-d124; over a minute on a populated one). The asker is one serial pass, so ops, board, balls, the transcript's re-read and the records' six queue behind it — every pass, because attention is asked again first. Opening /attention once has the same effect with nothing selected.

Seat side (this ball): a slow standing read must not starve the others — one slow question freezes every pane and the transcript with no word that anything is slow.

Why it matters: any operator whose selected conversation is asking — the normal case on a busy wall — gets panes that load half a minute late, and a transcript that updates twice a minute.
