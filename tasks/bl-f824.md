Observed twice in one session on this laptop (lane daily and its fork), each time on a tree that passed whole before and after: cargo test -q --lib exits with signal 11 partway through the 1400-test run, no test named. Suspects, unverified: the offscreen wgpu/lavapipe contexts the snapshot and window tests open in parallel threads (egui_kittest), or a test that drives the real child mint. Why it matters: a flake that kills the whole binary hides every result in the run and, on the builder, signs a FAIL for a good tree; a crash in a GL path may also be a crash a seat can hit. Next step: run with RUST_TEST_THREADS=1 and with --skip snapshot to bisect, and catch a core.

---

Caught under gdb (1 SIGSEGV in 5 debugger runs of the whole lib suite on the pre-fix tree): the faulting thread was snapshot::tests' matrix inside libvulkan.so.1 (egui_wgpu update_texture -> wgpu-hal create_texture -> vkSetDebugUtilsObjectNameEXT), while at the same instant ui::enroll::symbol::tests was in TestRenderer::new -> wgpu enumerate_adapters -> vkEnumeratePhysicalDevices -> dlclose. The system Vulkan loader unloads a driver library during enumeration while another thread calls through it: three tests each held a TestRenderer on parallel test threads. Fix: src/test_support/glass.rs, the one door to a renderer — created, used and dropped under one process-wide lock (renderer field drops before the guard); all three sites go through it. Rate is low: without a debugger the pre-fix binary passed 20/20 whole-suite runs and 40/40 renderer-only runs on this laptop, so the evidence is the captured backtrace, not a before/after count; post-fix 20/20 whole-suite runs clean. Ruled out: the hook-env GIT_DIR trap and SPAWN_LOCK (no child process in either stack), detached threads at exit (the crash was mid-run, not at teardown). Side note: under gdb ~20 channel::ladder tests fail with EINTR from the rendezvous DHT socket (debugger signals interrupt the recv); a debugger artifact, recorded here, not filed.
