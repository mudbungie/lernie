Gesture: yog fixture busy on an isolated engine; Ctrl+X, pick the wall, pick any conversation (all five ask); type /trail (or /board, /records) and Enter.

Expected: the pane fills within a beat or two, as it does when no conversation is selected (verified: /trail and /board answer with only the wall aimed).

Observed: 'waiting to hear what has crossed the boundary' (trail), 'waiting to hear what is on the board', and every records section, forever. Traced with a temporary print in the asker: the pass reads workspaces, then asks attention (it stands while the selected conversation asks, §4.42 item 8), and that read never returns; the asker is a serial pass, so ops, board, balls, the transcript's re-read and the records' six never go out. The line's read timeout is two minutes (channel::line::GONE) and the next pass asks attention again first.

Two causes, two homes. Engine side: lernie attention against a fresh busy fixture (yog 0.0.76) hangs past 20 s every time, with the engine holding the socket in CLOSE-WAIT — a yog defect. Seat side (this ball): a standing read that hangs must not starve the others; one slow question wedges every pane in the window with no notice that anything is wrong.

Why it matters: any operator whose selected conversation is asking — the normal case on a busy wall — gets panes that never load.
