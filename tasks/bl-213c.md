Twelve `control`-classed ops with no interactable here: `stop`, `interrupt`, `fork`, `delete-agent`, `retarget`, `agent`, `steps`, `step`, `files`, `rail`, `governing`, `inbox`.

The chat pane paints the transcript and the composer sends into it. Everything else an operator does TO a conversation — cutting it off mid-work, killing its driver, trying it again from a point in its history, deleting it, settling it onto another lineage — and everything they read ABOUT one — its own row, its steps, one step's records, its working files, its spine, the config commit it resolves policy from, the mail not yet delivered — reaches no gesture from this seat.

Exemption rows in `parity.toml` cite this ball until the surfaces land.
