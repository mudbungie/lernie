REMOTE §8.2 spends the leaf-to-host-name mapping at the channel boundary, over the workspace a gesture names. Which gestures name one is a table, and both ends have to agree about it or a gesture crosses under a name the far side never heard of.

The seat`s table (src/envelope.rs) mirrors yog`s exactly: the top-level `workspace` field, or `prepared.workspace` on the two envelopes that carry a prepared start. The two agree, which is the property that matters and which the suite pins.

They agree on an omission, though. The config family carries a workspace inside its destination — the wall a provider config belongs to — and neither table reads it as the gesture`s address. So a config act aimed at a workspace held on another box under a LOCAL rename resolves to no entry at all, goes to this box`s own engine, and edits the wrong wall`s file. yog`s own window has the same shape, so this is an upstream finding as much as a local one.

Two candidate answers and this ball picks one: read the destination`s workspace as the address (which makes the routing table read a nested field, and makes the config family route like everything else), or state outright that a config act is not addressed across a channel and refuse it there. Do not fix it on one side only — the tables agreeing is worth more than either answer.
