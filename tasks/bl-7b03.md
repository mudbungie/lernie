Round-1 daily lane. lernie 0.1.30, window under Xvfb at 1440x900.

The conversation pane's header is the literal word "conversation" and nothing else, on every conversation, in every state. `rounds/1/shots/daily/04-conversation.png`, `10-stopped-conv.png`.

Selected in `10-stopped-conv.png` is a conversation that is `stopped`, has four members, two steps wounded at the model's output cap, and has spent 5,089,466 tokens. The pane says "conversation". The only place any of that appears is the list row on the left — `ZucchiniFrost [stopped] 1m 4 waiting 4 members` — and the records pane two clicks away, which does carry all of it and carries it well:

    the conversation   ZucchiniFrost — stopped — at 0c7f76fb4cd0286bd53801124f6e1c15c27079ec
    marked notified, abandoned · ZucchiniFrost idle, CygnetMussel idle, StarlingEquinox idle, FrostyThunder idle
    5089466 tokens
    the engine offers nudge, stop with its children

So the facts are derived, typed, and already rendered somewhere — they are simply not on the surface the operator is looking at while reading the conversation. A person scrolling a transcript cannot tell whether they are reading something that is still running.

The same header is where the model would go. Nothing in the window says which model answered — `claude-sonnet-5` appears on each individual model row in the transcript, so the fact is present per-entry and absent as a property of the conversation, which is backwards for the question people actually ask ("what is this one running on?").

Expected: the header carries name, state, and spend, and says the model — the row's own `state`, `spend.tokens.total` and the role's model are all already in replies the seat holds. A `failure` clause belongs there too when there is one; today a conversation that died on a bad model id looks identical to one that finished.

Severity p3 by mechanism, but it is why nothing in the middle of the window can be trusted at a glance.
