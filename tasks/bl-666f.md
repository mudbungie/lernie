Gesture: a seat holding its own engine (wall 'home') and an entry 'side' whose workspace is also called 'home' on its host; /attention lists a row under each engine; press 'go to it' on the side engine's row.
Expected (4.19: 'a row is addressed off the ROSTER'): the side engine's home is aimed, its conversation selected.
Observed: the panel reads 'c-401 on home — (this box's own engine)', the rule 'the engine refused: unknown conversation "c-401"', and the ask box's answers would go to the own engine. Cause: Model::wall(workspace) (ui/model/queue.rs) walks the whole roster for the first wall with that name; the section the row came down (Asking::channel) is never consulted, and every answer door (post_seen, post_answer, go to it, the ask box) goes through it. Wall names are per engine ('home' is every fresh engine's bootstrap name), so this collides on any seat with two engines.
Why it matters: an answer to a parked call is an act — sent to the wrong engine it is at best refused and at worst lands on a same-named conversation there.
