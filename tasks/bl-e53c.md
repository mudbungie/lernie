Three reply shapes sit in `corpus/unreadable/` — `clients`, `invocations` and `agent` — and under the corpus README that directory is a ledger rather than a defect list: *"a kind that ought to be painted and sits here is a filed ball, not an oversight."* This is that ball.

PROTOCOL 2 (yog bl-77be) grew two of them a field the seat therefore cannot show:

- `reply/clients` rows now carry `/rows/[]/tools/[]/subject_cwd:bool` — a box per-tool consent to execute at a working directory the invocation names (yog REMOTE §5.1 as amended). It is the worktree lane routing fact, and an operator with no way to see which entries consent cannot tell a foot that will take a subject from one that will refuse it.
- `reply/invocations` rows now carry `/rows/[]/cwd:string` — where the subject was, honoured only under that consent (§5.3).

Nothing is broken today: rung 4 of the decode policy rides an unread field through untouched, and the ledger states plainly that this build does not paint the surface. What is missing is the pane.

The work is a surface, not a decoder: a client roster showing each connected foot, its advertised tools and, per tool, whether it consents to a named subject_cwd — plus the invocation rows with the cwd each ran at. Landing it moves those two files out of `unreadable/` into `answers/`, and the diff of that move is the record of exactly what the release added.

Filed at close of bl-5b07, which bumped the seat to PROTOCOL 2 and found the two fields land on shapes no pane reads.
