Filed while re-vendoring the wire to PROTOCOL 13. Two ops classed `control`
in yog's help roster reach nothing in this seat:

    /pin      float this workspace to the front of the strip and keep it there
    /unpin    take this workspace back out of the pinned run

They are workspace-level acts on the strip, not conversation acts, so the
right-click menu at the conversation row is the wrong home for them.

Until a control exists, both carry a line in `parity.toml` citing this ball.
Building the control deletes those two lines and changes no config, which is
the severability test that file states.
