## Concern
The workspace-wide startup scan (§2.11) runs at every driver invocation — with the §6 exec baton, every hop of every chain re-sweeps every branch, and the scan's inbox flush launches drivers that themselves scan. Frequency is matched to step rate; the events it compensates for happen at crash rate. O(branches) per spawn, driver-launching-driver recursion.

## Baseline: the minimal design already carries normal operation
The front door alone is self-propagating: `lernie message` (1) deposits, (2) probes the executor lock, (3) launches a driver if quiescent; the executor drains its inbox at every step boundary. Deposit-before-probe ordering means a queued item exists before anyone checks. Every terminal event deposits (§2.3 step 5 is total), so the healthy path needs zero scanning and zero inbox-watching.

## What the minimal design does NOT answer (the scan's real job)
Exactly one class: **uncatchable death** (SIGKILL, OOM, panic, power loss mid-chain), two symptoms:
(a) a crashed child never deposits its result — its parked parent waits for a deposit that never comes (the died-epitaph sweep exists for this, §8);
(b) messages deposited while the doomed executor lived — writer's probe saw the lock held, so no launch; executor crashed before the drain; files sit undelivered with nobody to launch (same stranding if the launched driver itself dies before acquiring, e.g. reboot between deposit and drain).
Plus one race inside graceful exit: a deposit landing between the executor's final drain and its lock release — probe sees lock held, no launch, executor exits without draining.

## Proposed segmentation
1. **Executor** (step loop): no scan. Drop the startup scan from `lernie dispatch` re-entries and advance baton hops. One addition closes the graceful-exit race *inside* the minimal design: the executor's exit path, after releasing the lock, runs the same probe-and-launch a writer does against its own inbox (drain → release → list → launch-if-pending). With that, graceful delivery is total with no scan at all.
2. **Reviver** (`lernie message` deposit path): unchanged — the minimal design above.
3. **Janitor**: the workspace-wide sweep+flush (existing code, src/prompt/inbox/scan/) becomes its own verb (`lernie scan`), invoked at crash-rate-appropriate points only: user-facing entry (`lernie prompt` — preserves §2.11 first-touch-heals) and an optional cron heartbeat. Optionally, a driver starting for agent X sweeps only X's direct children (prefix walk of X-*, same namespace walk as bl-535d) — the exact set of deposits X could be stalled on, checked when X is driven.
