ARCH 2.1 (bl-1966) rules that in any document that numbers its own sections, a bare section number is that document own section, and every reference to another document section names the document. docs/DESIGN_MCP_BRIDGE.md and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md comply. docs/TAXONOMY.md (1..12 of its own) and docs/USER_STORIES.md (1..12 of its own) do not: both cite ARCHITECTURE 2.3 / 3.3 / 2.11 etc. with a bare number, and TAXONOMY own section 4 (Tools, function calling, MCP) collides head-on with the ARCH section 4 it also cites. ~250 sites across the two files; each needs a per-site judgement (self vs ARCHITECTURE), so it is a sweep, not a sed. Deliverable: both files comply and each states the convention at its head, as DESIGN_MCP_BRIDGE does.
