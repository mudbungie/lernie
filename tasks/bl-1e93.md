Latent race, fired live on the 0.0.1 cut (run 30231615367): the release-binaries job resolves its tag via [ -n "$tag" ] || tag="$(git describe --tags --abbrev=0)" against the RUN's checkout SHA, while the release job publishes from the BRANCH TIP. When main advances between the two (here: a concurrent landing put the tag on a descendant of the run's SHA), git describe fails — 'fatal: No tags can describe 524986a...' — and the binaries job dies. Worked around by the designed workflow_dispatch backfill (-f binaries_tag=v0.0.1, run 30231841897, green). Fix: the binaries job should take the tag from the release job's OUTPUT (release-plz knows what it minted) instead of re-deriving from a checkout that may predate the tag. Will recur on any release cut that races a concurrent close.
