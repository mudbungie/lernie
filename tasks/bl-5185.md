Round-1 multitenant lane.

## The page

    $ lernie help follow
    usage: lernie follow <workspace> <agent>

    hold the line on one conversation's live tail

    A read that deliberately never finishes: the connection stays open and
    the engine writes a frame every time the tail moves. Each frame is the
    WHOLE accumulated fold rather than a delta, so a frame missed is nothing
    missed. It ends when the engine ends it, or when this end hangs up.

## The wire

    $ lernie follow alpha CarvingColander
    {"kind":"follow","ok":true,"stream":{"delta":"text","text":"The"}}
    {"kind":"follow","ok":true,"stream":{"delta":"text","text":" essay is complete at roughly 3,200 words, well past the 2,000-word minimum, written across several passes covering:\n\n1"}}
    {"kind":"follow","ok":true,"stream":{"delta":"text","text":". **Introduction** — framing the essay's scope\n2. **Ancient Calendars** …"}}

Each frame carries only what landed since the previous one. Concatenating them
yields the answer; treating any one of them as the whole fold yields a
fragment.

## The authority

yog REMOTE §5.5 (bl-3655) is explicit that this is the ruling and not a drift:

    **A `Query::Follow` frame carries what landed since that read's previous
    frame, not the whole answer.** This is the ruling a seat implements
    against, and it is one rule with no flag and no case:

    > Absorb every frame of a read, in order, onto an empty fold. What you
    > hold after the last frame you have received is what you paint.

and names the hazard this page is an instance of:

    **The wire spelling did not move, and that is the hazard worth stating.**
    … the two text fields are now the *appended* part rather than the
    accumulated one.

## Expected

The page states the append rule and the "absorb onto an empty fold" instruction,
since a person reading `lernie follow`'s raw output is exactly the audience the
old sentence misleads — it tells them a missed frame is nothing missed, when a
missed frame is missed text.

The rest of the page is accurate: `follow` does hold the line (a 15 s timeout
killed it rather than it exiting) and it does end when the engine ends the
turn.

## Severity

p3. Nothing misbehaves; the help page is simply the opposite of the protocol
and would be believed by anyone building against this seat's output.
