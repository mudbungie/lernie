## 1. `reply/follow` gained `tools` (yog bl-5305)

The follow lane carried the model's prose alone, so an operator watching an
agent administer their machines saw a thinking marker and two sentences while
eight commands ran on two boxes. The frame body now carries the **tool
window** beside the fold, appended by the same rule the prose obeys —
concatenate the lists of a read's frames in order:

    {"tool_use": "toolu_01", "tool": "box2_Bash",
     "input": "{\"command\":\"hostname && uptime\"}"}
    {"tool_use": "toolu_01", "exit_code": 0}

- Two entries per call and both are transitions: `input.json` lands before the
  call is dispatched, `output.json` when the capture returns.
- `exit_code`'s **presence** is the status. Absent is a call in flight.
- The closing entry restates neither name nor input — key it by `tool_use`.
- The machine a routed call ran on is already in `tool`: REMOTE §5.1 presents a
  loaded remote tool as `<client>_<tool>`, always.
- `tools` is **required**, empty list included.

The lane's liveness also widened: a follow read now follows a STEP rather than
the model call inside it, so it stays open through the tool phase instead of
terminating the instant a call settles into its `tool_use` blocks.

## 2. `reply/ops` rows gained `client` (yog bl-e59e, round-1 ruling 5)

Every trail row now names the identity that made the act — a connection's
certificate common name, or `local` for the window, the gestures inbox and
yog's own loops. Read strictly, like `standing`: it is the second field a
reader cannot recompute from the line beside it.

## What this ball asks of lernie

1. Re-vendor `corpus/` from yog and make the conformance replay pass on both
   shapes, in both directions.
2. `lernie follow` renders the tool window: what is running, where, and what it
   came back with. Prose-only output is the defect this closes.
3. The ops rendering names the author.
4. **`lernie help follow` is wrong today** and yog bl-5305 filed it as the
   second half of that ball: it states "Each frame is the WHOLE accumulated
   fold rather than a delta, so a frame missed is nothing missed." REMOTE §5.5
   has said the opposite since bl-3655 — a frame carries what landed since the
   read's previous frame, and a follower absorbs them in order onto an empty
   fold. Either the help text or the frames were wrong, and it is the help
   text.
