ARCHITECTURE §2.11 'The startup scan' / §8: every driver invocation (lernie prompt/dispatch/advance), before anything else, runs one workspace-wide scan with two derived actions. (1) Sweep: enumerate agents/* branches with no live executor (lock probe) that died mid-work (response.json closed without terminal end) or, for a child, never deposited a result message; for each hard-crashed child, deposit the died-epitaph result message on the child's behalf — sender is the child (scribe, not author). (2) Flush: list inbox/*/*; every agent with pending files and a free lock gets a driver launched — launched, never drained; only the lock-holding executor delivers. Transient process, no watcher, no schedule; an idle workspace stays unswept until the next invocation by design (operator remedy: cron a lernie advance — config, not schema).
