## What is being asked for

The engine grows an act — an enroll, naming a box and a grade (`foot` or `operator`) — answered by a reply kind carrying the material for that box: its grade, its name, the address it dials, the trust anchor, its leaf and that leaf's key. The seat sends the act over an entry it already holds at operator grade, and shows the answer as a QR symbol the operator points a camera at.

The seat gains a verb and a window control on an entry. The encoder is bl-ad61 and is not this ball's work; this ball is the act, the reading of the answer, and where the answer goes.

## Why this is not the in-channel bootstrap §1.4 forbids

It is worth writing down before any of it is built, because it looks exactly like the thing that must never exist.

REMOTE §1.4 forbids a box acquiring its identity **over its own channel** — no pairing, no first-connect ceremony, no button that hands an unauthenticated dialer a certificate. Nothing here does that. The act crosses a channel that is **already** authenticated, at **operator** grade, on material an operator carried by hand; the box being enrolled is not a party to it and has no channel yet. What reaches the new box reaches it out of channel, through a camera pointed at a screen — the same class of act as carrying a file on a stick, and mediated by the same operator.

lernie's DESIGN §3 says the seat *"never mints"*, and it still does not: the mint is the engine's, on the engine's box, over a trust root that already exists. The seat asks and paints. That section names enrollment as a thing with no flow in the channel, and it needs the amendment that says which of those two sentences was the invariant and which was a statement about the surface as it then stood.

## The material is displayed and then gone

The seat never writes it down. Not to its own state, not to a cache, not to a temporary file, not to a log line. It is held for as long as a symbol is on screen and it is dropped with the pane.

The reason is the whole point of the flow: this is the one moment a seat holds a private key that is not its own, for a box that does not exist yet, and a seat is not a certificate authority's spool. Anything persisted is a credential at rest that nobody chose to store and nobody will remember to delete.

**Test it as an absence.** Drive the act against the stand-in engine over a throwaway root and assert that no file appears anywhere under it — the assertion is over the tree, not over the paths the code happens to know about, because a defect here is precisely a path nobody thought of.
