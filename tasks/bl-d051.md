Seen on the verification lane for bl-5369 (DESIGN 4.46 item 3): Ctrl+X then Ctrl+, leaves the switcher on the glass; Esc then reveals the settings pane that the chord opened underneath. The switcher is a flag beside the lookups, and overlay::stand tries the switcher first, so two can be open at once with one painted. Candidate: a chord or word that opens a pane puts the switcher down, as opening the switcher puts down whatever overlay stood (4.42 item 7, bl-339d).
