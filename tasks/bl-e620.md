## Already landed — do not rebuild it

- **A row this seat holds no name for is painted, and painted as unreachable.**
  `ui::Channel::address`'s third case and `ui::roster::NO_NAME_HERE`, ruled in
  DESIGN §4.11: dropping it would hide a workspace the operator has, and
  addressing it by the entry's leaf would aim a gesture at a different wall.
  That is the row-state shape the original asked for.
- **A refusal is one entry's, never the set's** (DESIGN §4.6). An entry carries
  its own `Result`, so a half-provisioned one says so while every other stands,
  and the union asker proves it — *"a channel that will not answer leaves the
  others standing"*.
- **A box with no channel at all names the operator's act** rather than
  reporting an absence (`ui::roster::NO_CHANNEL`).

## The live defect

A channel that cannot be OPENED, or that will not ANSWER, reaches the model as
`Said::Unreachable` and lands in `Model::notice` — the shell-wide bar across the
top, which is where a fact about the whole window goes. Two consequences:

- the sentence never says WHICH channel it is about, so a seat holding three
  cannot tell from the glass which relationship is down;
- one dead channel writes over the notice a live one just earned, because there
  is one notice and it belongs to the model rather than to a chunk.

The original's rule is the fix and survives verbatim: **an unreachable channel
paints its sentence on its own rows, never the whole shell.** The roster is
already grouped by the channel each row came down, so the seat exists; what is
missing is a per-chunk state and the routing of `unreachable` into it instead of
into the bar.

Keep the bar for what the bar is for — a refusal the ENGINE said about a
gesture, and a frame this seat could not read. Those are facts about an
exchange; this is a fact about a relationship, and DESIGN §4.11 already draws
that line for the other two.

## Two bullets of the original dropped, and why

- **"Pane facts stay local — sweep every writer of pane keys."** This crate
  writes no pane document at all yet. Where per-seat UI state lives is bl-0fba's
  whole subject, and filing it twice would give one fact two homes.
- **"World facts ride the workspace's channel", and attention ranking across
  channels.** Neither act exists here: the verb table is six rows and none of
  them writes a world fact, and there is no attention ranking. When such a
  gesture is built it routes per channel by construction — `seat::route` is the
  only place the leaf-to-host mapping is spent (DESIGN §4.7) — so it is a
  property to assert at that act's own landing, not work to schedule now.

## Discipline

Paint assertions through `crate::paint_probe` only
(`rules/no-hand-rolled-paint-walk`). Run `make line-cap LINE_CAP=199` before
extending the roster module, and split along the chunk-state seam if the
projection passes 200.

## Driven live on 0.1.0 — both consequences confirmed, and a third

Reproduced in the window over a scratch data root holding a working flat
channel plus two entries that cannot answer: one addressing a port nothing
listens on, one whose anchor did not sign the engine's certificate.

**The sentence names no channel — it says "it".** The bar painted, verbatim:

    ×  this seat could not reach it: connect <address>: Connection refused (os error 111)

`it`. The only identifying thing in the line is the address, which is not the
channel's name and is not unique — two entries against one host would be
indistinguishable, and the seat's own naming (the entry leaf, and the host
spelling under a §8.2 rename) never appears. The first bullet above understates
this: the sentence does not merely fail to say which of three, it names no
subject at all.

**Only one of the two failures was ever heard.** Over three captures spanning
several beats the bar held the same connect error every time; the wrong-anchor
channel failed on every beat too and produced no sentence anywhere in the
window, ever. So the second bullet's *"writes over the notice a live one just
earned"* is the mild case; the standing case is that a seat with two dead
channels is told about one of them, permanently, and cannot discover the other
from the glass at all.

**And the dismiss is inert, which is new.** `crate::ui::shell::DISMISS` is
documented as *"An operator who has read it should not have to wait for the
next answer to clear it."* Three clicks on `×`, two and a half seconds apart,
left the identical sentence standing each time — because the roster is re-read
on the beat and the same failure re-posts faster than a human can clear it. A
relationship that is down is down on every beat, so for exactly the class of
notice this ball is about, the only control on the bar does nothing. Routing
the fact to the chunk dissolves that too: a row's state is not something one
dismisses.

## One correction to "Already landed"

The third bullet — *"A box with no channel at all names the operator's act
(`ui::roster::NO_CHANNEL`)"* — is not true on a real box. `crate::seat::channels`
always answers a chunk for the flat slot, so `model.roster.is_empty()` is false
and that constant paints only in a unit test; what an unprovisioned box actually
gets is a section header with an empty body. Filed separately as bl-08b6, since
its fix is the pane's empty-SECTION state rather than the routing of an
unreachable. The two meet: a channel that failed paints the same empty section,
and both want a sentence per section rather than per roster.
