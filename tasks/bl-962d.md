Before §2.11, a subagent's output reached its parent only through compacted merge-back — a narrow, summarized channel. Front-door messaging lets any conversation inject arbitrary context into any live branch of the tree, including the root: a prompt-injected child steering its parent is now one tool call instead of a compaction escape. Provenance (from: frontmatter, harness-derived sender) is recorded and unforgeable, and the single-user assumption (§1.1) covers authorization — but not influence.
