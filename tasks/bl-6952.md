## The sighting

The installed seat at 0.1.4, connected to a protocol-6 engine on the same box
(yog 0.0.9, over the loopback wire), paints a **giant black box in the middle
of the app**. Reported by the operator against a running window.

Screen capture is blocked on that desktop, so every finding below comes from
driving the real panes headlessly over that same live engine — the seat's own
reader, the seat's own `ui::render`, real protocol-6 answers off the wire.

## Two of the three candidate classes are eliminated

**It is not a 0.1.3 -> 0.1.4 paint regression.** The published diff between the
two tags touches exactly two files: `src/channel/hello.rs` — the `PROTOCOL`
integer and its doc comment — and `docs/DESIGN.md`. **No paint code, no model
code and no reply code changed at all.** 0.1.4 cannot have introduced a paint
defect relative to 0.1.3, because it contains no paint change.

**It is not a shape that moved under the protocol-6 re-vendor.** All three
kinds this window reads were fetched from the live engine and put through the
seat's own decoder: `workspaces`, `conversations` and `transcript` each decode
as `Read::Answer` carrying real rows. Every reply shape the two bumps touched —
`governing`, `providers`, `agent`, `attention` — is one this seat does not
decode at all and files under `corpus/unreadable/`, so no decoded shape moved.

**What 0.1.4 actually changed is that the seat can connect at all.** 0.1.2
spoke protocol 4 and 0.1.3 spoke 5; the engine speaks 6 and the handshake is
fail-closed with no negotiation. So 0.1.4 is the **first build in this chain
whose panes have ever held data from this engine**. Whatever this is, the
evidence says it is first-*seen*, not newly-*introduced* — which is a different
defect with a different owner, and worth knowing before anything is bisected.

## The resting window does not reproduce it

The full shell was rendered over the largest real conversation on the box (27
entries, 34 chat rows) at four window sizes — 1400x900, 1000x700, 700x500 and
1920x1080 — and every filled rectangle on the glass was measured. At every
size the only near-black region is the composer's own text box: 44 points tall,
along the bottom edge. Everything else is the toolkit's dark ground (27,27,27),
selection blue, or text. **Nothing black, nothing giant, at any size.**

## The one surface in this seat that paints true black

There is exactly one, and the whole tree has no other filled rectangle outside
the toolkit: the enrollment pane's QR symbol (`src/ui/enroll.rs`, `theme::INK`
is `Color32::BLACK`). Three facts about it, measured:

- it is the **one pane that covers the middle of the window** — it replaces the
  central panel by design, and the module doc says so;
- a real §8.4 envelope measures 1681 bytes, which is a 153-module symbol drawn
  as a **644-point square** at about half its modules dark;
- it is only ever on screen after the operator opens `enroll a box…`, and 0.1.4
  is the first build that could have completed one against this engine.

That makes it the leading candidate on shape, position and timing at once — but
it is a candidate, not a finding, because nothing here can say which pane the
operator was looking at. Its measured legibility problem is filed separately as
**bl-5e0e**, which stands on its own numbers whether or not it is this.

Eliminated along the way, so nobody re-runs it: the symbol tessellates to a
single 95,524-vertex mesh, which crosses the u16 index ceiling — but
`egui_glow` 0.29.1 draws with `glow::UNSIGNED_INT`, so that is not a fault
here. This build links **glow, not wgpu** (`Cargo.toml`, `default-features =
false` with `default_fonts` and `glow`), so a wgpu diagnosis would be aimed at
a backend this binary does not contain.

## What landed

**The harness gained the projection this investigation needed.**
`crate::paint_probe` was a walk over painted *text* only, so *"is there a large
dark block on the glass, and which pane emits it"* was a question it could not
answer — and answering it meant a second walk, which
`rules/no-hand-rolled-paint-walk.yml` exists to refuse. The one walk now
carries filled rectangles beside glyphs and `fills_of` is their projection,
tested in both directions.

**No "there is no black box" assertion was added**, deliberately. It would pass
today, would have passed before the sighting, and would go on passing whatever
happened next — a beat that proves nothing, which is the shape this repo's own
`beat-audit` exists to reject.

Also found and filed rather than smuggled in here: **bl-beb7**, a committed
model entry with an empty thinking block painting a blank row, which the live
path's own stated rule forbids. Reproduced on live engine data.

## The one question for the operator

Everything above narrows to a single fact this end cannot obtain:

> **When the black box is on screen, is the "enroll a box" pane open — that is,
> does the window show the heading `enroll a box` with `done — forget it`
> above the black area, and does the black area disappear when you click that
> control?**

Yes puts it on bl-5e0e and the QR is the subject. No eliminates the only
surface in this seat that paints black at all, and the next step is the
toolkit rather than the seat — at which point the useful follow-up is whether
the box survives resizing the window.
