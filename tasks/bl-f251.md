After bl-73d2/bl-d1ae/bl-2d6b (docs/STYLE.md, the six-state palette, no bordered panes) the window is cleaner but still spartan at 1440×900: 13pt body everywhere, the composer is a thin bar with a row of chip-buttons (send interrupt nudge / records… stop revoke restore retarget / flag delete) that read as a form, the conversation pane is mostly empty ground with no visual anchor, and the roster's window-level acts are a list of prose links. Second pass, in this order: (1) the composer is the pane's focal element — a taller field at the bottom with the primary act (send) inside it and the state glow, secondary acts (interrupt/nudge/stop) as a compact icon+label row that only shows what the conversation offers, destructive acts (revoke/retarget/delete) behind one '…' menu with their typed-name arming; (2) a real conversation header: name at HEADING size, state as a coloured word, model and spend as weak ink on one line, the subtree path if any; (3) type scale: body 14, transcript entries with generous line height, entries separated by spacing not rules; (4) the roster's window acts become a compact top strip (icons or short verbs), the wall's act band the same; (5) empty states say what to do next in one line. Screenshots before/after under Xvfb into rounds/3/shots/design-desktop/iteration-2/.
