The engine's wire moved twice and this seat still said 2. yog's next release
refuses a protocol-2 seat fail-closed, so a seat that does not speak 4 stops
being able to dial at all — this is the client half of the release-chain
rebuild the operator ordered on 2026-09-01.

What moved upstream, both bumps in one unreleased cycle (REMOTE §9.10, §9.11):

- **3 — the failure clause.** `reply/conversations`' row, the §6 queue row and
  the `agent` answer gained `failure`: the first clause of why the
  conversation's latest model call failed. Absent-not-null on the conversation
  row, `null` on the queue row. `tone: "bad"` widened at the same time — it
  used to fire only for an auth-shaped refusal and now fires for any failed
  latest call — which is a widening of when a value is true, not a change to
  what it says, so no signature moved for it.
- **4 — the flag.** The §6 queue row gained `flag` (when a flag was raised and
  why, in the raiser's words) and `signals` gained the token `flagged`.

## What landed

- `corpus/` re-vendored through `scripts/refresh-corpus.sh`, the only
  sanctioned way it moves. **Nothing was added or retired**: the request
  direction and the shape key set came back identical, and six reply fixtures
  plus `shapes.json` moved. Two of the six are this bump (`conversations`,
  `attention`); `agent` is the same bump on a shape filed under the ledger; the
  other three (`files`, `help`, `search`) are unrelated upstream edits the
  vendor had been behind on and all three sit in the ledger already.
- `PROTOCOL` 2 -> 4 in `src/channel/hello.rs`, with both bumps recorded at the
  constant.
- `failure` carried into `ConvRow` and painted: the clause hangs under the
  row's headline, **above** the preview, in the row's own tone. Above, because
  the preview is what was last said and the clause is why nothing more was, so
  a row reads label, reason, last words. Neither the hue nor the clause is
  derived from the other — the engine states them separately and a seat that
  inferred one would be holding a second opinion about a reading it never took.
- The two paint blocks under a row were one shape written twice, so they are
  one function now (`ui::convs::beneath`).
- DESIGN §4.9 records the rule the bump is the worked example of: **the number
  moves for the wire, the fields move for the panes.** Three of the four new
  fields ride through unread and their shapes stay in `corpus/unreadable/`,
  which is the ledger working rather than a shortfall.

`last_active_unix` (REMOTE §9.9, PROTOCOL 2) was already vendored and stays
unread, recorded at `reply::convs`' module doc: this pane paints *how long ago*
and nothing paints *when*.
