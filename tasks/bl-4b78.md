Gesture: Ctrl+X, arrows to a conversation, Enter, then type. Expected: the switcher was opened to talk to something; after Enter the operator is where they talk — the prompt holds the caret (as '+ new' already puts it there, §4.42 item 7). Observed: the overlay closes, nothing is focused, the typed words are dropped; one Tab reaches the box. The exception that must stay: while the chosen conversation is asking, the ask's digits answer only while nothing holds the keyboard (keys::asking), so the caret must not be taken there. Why it matters: keyboard-only operation is a promise (README, QUALITY F1), and silent dropped keystrokes are the worst way to break it.
