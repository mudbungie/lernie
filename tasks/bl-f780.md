`crate::ui::convs` has two things to say about an empty pane — `NO_WALL`
(*"pick a workspace"*) when nothing is aimed at, and `NO_CONVERSATIONS`
(*"no conversations here"*) otherwise. There is no third, so a wall this
window has not been ANSWERED about is painted with the sentence reserved for a
wall that answered nothing.

**The pane already knows better about a row and does not apply it to itself.**
`crate::ui::convs::headline` is explicit:

> **The badge wears a `?` for a state nothing observed.** The engine answers a
> reading and whether it could take one, and painting the first without the
> second would state a definite fact about a conversation nobody looked at.

*No conversations here* is a definite fact about a WALL nobody looked at. The
same argument, one level up.

Two ways to reach it, and the second does not go away.

**Transient, on every aim change.** `Model::aim_at` clears `convs`, so between
the keypress and the answer the pane paints the new wall's name over an empty
list. Driven headless against a loopback engine: aiming from a wall with no
conversations to one holding five — one of them in flight, four of them waiting
— the pane painted

    conversations
    <the new wall>
    no conversations here

for six consecutive captured frames before the five rows arrived. On a loopback
engine that is about a second; over the wire it is the whole round trip, and a
seat is for the far case.

**Permanent, out of a saved place.** `crate::place` restores an aim without
checking it, on the stated ground that *"a stale place is inert by a rule that
was already there ... the standing set already declines to ask about one."*
Inert is right about the DIALLING and wrong about the PAINT. With a
`place.json` naming a channel and address this box holds no material for, the
window opens aimed at that wall and paints

    conversations
    <the saved wall>
    no conversations here

and — because nothing will ever be asked — keeps painting it. No roster row is
selected, no notice stands, and nothing distinguishes it from a wall that
genuinely holds nothing. The one state `place` is documented to make harmless
is the one where the falsehood is permanent.

**What is wanted.** The list needs the `?` doctrine: rows that are the answer to
a stated aim, and a third sentence for the aim that has not been answered.
A wall the seat cannot resolve at all deserves better than either — it is the
`crate::state::Standing` refusal, and it should be legible rather than silent.

Found by driving the window headless (X server, screenshots read back).
